This site is designed to collect as little information about its visitors as practical. It does not use advertising trackers, sell visitor information, or use analytics cookies.

## Analytics

This site uses a self-hosted instance of [Plausible Analytics](https://plausible.io/) to understand aggregate traffic patterns, such as popular pages, referral sources, browser types, and approximate countries.

Plausible is designed to operate without cookies and without tracking people across websites or devices. Analytics data is used only to understand and improve this site.

For more information, see the [Plausible data policy](https://plausible.io/data-policy).

## Comments

Comments are provided by [Giscus](https://giscus.app/) and stored as public discussions in the site's GitHub repository.

When a page containing comments loads, or when you sign in and leave a comment, GitHub and Giscus may process information according to their own policies. Comments, GitHub usernames, profile images, and reactions are public.

For more information, see the [GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Local Preferences

If you use the theme button, your light or dark theme preference is stored in your browser's local storage. This preference remains on your device and is not sent to this site.

## Hosting

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub and the infrastructure used to operate this site may process technical request information, such as IP addresses and browser details, for security, reliability, and service operation.

## External Links

This site contains links to external websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

## Questions

For privacy questions about this site, contact me through [GitHub](https://github.com/danohn).

This policy may be updated when the site's services or privacy practices change.
