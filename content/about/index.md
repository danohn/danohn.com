Hi, I'm Daniel. I'm a homelabber and builder. I spend a lot of my spare time setting up clusters, self-hosting services, and figuring out how AI can help me learn and understand the systems underneath them.

I write here about what I find along the way: Kubernetes, infrastructure, automation, developer workflows, and using LLMs to learn, debug, and write code.

Most posts start with something I'm actually trying to do. A service breaks, a cluster behaves strangely, or I build a small tool because the existing options don't quite fit. LLMs are usually somewhere in that process, acting as a pair programmer, tutor, or rubber duck. This site is where I keep the useful bits.
